About This Calendar

What's included:

  • Startup and entrepreneurship events (pitch competitions, demo days, founder meetups, accelerator programs)
  • Startup training (workshops, bootcamps, SBIR/STTR and grant-writing training)
  • Investor events (angel conferences, venture showcases)
  • Biotech, bioscience, life science, medtech, and digital health industry events
  • Programs supporting women and underrepresented founders

How to subscribe:

Click the "Subscribe to Calendar" button above and choose your option:

  • Google Calendar: Click "Subscribe in Google Calendar" for automatic updates when new events are added
  • Outlook/Apple Calendar: Click "Download for Outlook/Apple" and import the .ics file into your calendar app

Coverage: Events in Oregon and Southwest Washington (Clark, Cowlitz, Skamania, Wahkiakum, Pacific, and Klickitat counties)

Updates: This calendar is updated weekly with new events from verified sources across the region.

Suggest an event: Know of an event we should include? Click the "Suggest an Event" button above to submit it for review.
